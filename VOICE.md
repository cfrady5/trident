# Voice and messaging

From the brand guide (p.12–16). Applies to the website, emails, product UI copy and every AI drafting workflow (these rules are part of the system prompts for production workflows).

> We sound like the expert in the room who also knows how to sell. Calm, exact and commercially minded. We respect the science and we respect the buyer's time.

## Traits

| Trait | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Precision-driven | Specific. Real numbers, named outcomes, exact status language. | Vague, padded or approximate where a fact exists. |
| Sophisticated | Confident and restrained. Short sentences, no exclamation marks. | Flashy, hyped, full of buzzwords. |
| Ambitious | Forward-looking. We describe the market a client can win. | Grandiose, or promising outcomes we can't control. |
| Technical | Fluent in the technology and the program world; we translate both. | Exclusionary. Acronyms are spelled out on first use. |
| Entrepreneurial | Commercial and action-oriented. Every piece points to a next step. | Bureaucratic, passive or process for its own sake. |

## Mechanics

- **Case:** sentence case for headlines. Statements end with a full stop: *Beyond the Award.*
- **Commas:** no serial comma — *brands, digital experiences and growth strategies.*
- **Numbers:** numerals for data, measurements and ranges (3 posts, $4.2M). Spell out one to nine only in running prose. Always give the year a figure covers.
- **Acronyms:** spell out on first use, then the acronym — *Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR)*.
- **Name:** Trident in running text. TRIDENT appears only in logo artwork.
- **Punctuation:** no exclamation marks. No emoji in brand channels.
- **UI copy:** say what happens next ("We reply within two business days"), never "Oops" or "Something awesome".

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Your Phase II proves the technology works. Now prove it sells. | Unlock transformative value with synergistic go-to-market solutions! |
| Selected as Awardable on the Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace. | Awarded a Tradewinds contract. |
| Your positioning brief lands on [date]. One review round, then final. | We'll circle back ASAP once we've aligned internally. |
| Built for dual-use buyers: defense programs and commercial operators. | The Pentagon's go-to marketing partner. |
| We cut the deck from 24 slides to 11. The ask is on slide 2. | We created an amazing, best-in-class deck experience. |

## Say / don't say (guardrails)

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| Selected as Awardable on the [marketplace name], [month year]. | Won a contract on [marketplace]. Government-approved vendor. |
| SBIR Phase II awardee, [agency], [year]. | Endorsed by [agency]. Backed by the government. |
| Works with companies across federal innovation programs. | Official partner of [program]. Program-approved agency. |
| Government-validated. Market-ready. (as our positioning line) | "Government-validated" about a client with no named, verifiable credential. |

Before publishing anything: Is every claim verified? Is status language exact? Does it name an agency or show a mark? Does it imply a partnership?

## Messaging hierarchy

| Level | Copy | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Tagline | Beyond the Award. | KIT v1.0 |
| Secondary | Government-validated. Market-ready. | PROPOSAL |
| Promise | Turning breakthrough technology into market opportunity. | KIT v1.0 |
| Headline | Innovation deserves a market. | KIT v1.0 |
| Method | Position · Build · Activate | KIT v1.0 |

Messaging pillars (PROPOSAL): **Validation into demand** · **Fluent in both worlds** · **Independent by design** (provisional — gate). Proof points are `[CONFIRM]`; none are invented on the site.

Headline bank (PROPOSAL): *Proven isn't the same as found.* · *Your award opened the door. We build what's behind it.* · *From awardable to adopted.* · *The technology passed review. Now the market gets a vote.*

## Ready-to-use copy (PROPOSAL)

- **One line:** Trident turns government-validated technology into commercial market opportunity.
- **50 / 100-word boilerplates:** guide p.15 (contain `[CONFIRM: website]` / `[CONFIRM: location]`; do not publish until filled).
- **Standard disclaimer:** Trident is an independent company. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government agency or program.

## Claims policy for product features

- The **Commercial Readiness Index** is always described as *Trident's own preliminary assessment methodology — not a government certification, an endorsement or a validated industry benchmark*.
- No fake metrics, testimonials, client logos or case studies. The case-study type exists (`src/content/case-studies.ts`) with zero entries; entries render only with written client approval and sourced outcomes.
- Commercialization outcomes are never promised. Market figures are quoted as the publisher's finding with source, measure and year.
